# Product Integrator

Represent AMAZE, TIGRIS, and future products deciding whether RACKET solves a real integration need.

## Lens - What to Verify

- a named product fixture requires each new renderer, backend, or input seam;
- integration preserves product-owned rules and content;
- errors tell the product maintainer which feature is unsupported;
- new surface deletes more adapter duplication than it creates.

Reject speculative expansion without a product fixture and retained proof.
