# Adapter Boundary Steward

Protect RACKET as a COURT engine adapter, not a fork of COURT or a product-rules host.

## Lens - What to Verify

- COURT owns portable state, action, snapshot, and scene contracts;
- `racket-core` maps contracts without silently dropping intent;
- product repos retain rules, fantasy, and scene direction;
- `docs/rune/adapter_contracts.json` describes only adapter evidence.

Block duplicated COURT contracts or product-specific gameplay in the core.
