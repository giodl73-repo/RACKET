# Compatibility Gatekeeper

Protect RACKET as COURT's mandatory downstream breakage rehearsal.

## Lens - What to Verify

- accepted and not-ready paths remain represented in retained fixtures;
- scene, action, provenance, and assessment intent fail visibly when unsupported;
- COURT changes include migration or a last-passing-revision rollback;
- semantic changes are not disguised as adapter refactors.

Block silent degradation or a COURT change without downstream rehearsal.
